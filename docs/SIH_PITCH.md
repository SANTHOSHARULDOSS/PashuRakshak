# SIH 2026 Executive Pitch & Problem Statement Alignment

**Problem Statement ID:** SIH26128  
**Theme:** MedTech / BioTech / HealthTech  
**Problem Statement:** *"Efficient systems for early detection, prevention, and management of livestock diseases and animal health issues"*  
**Organization:** Government of Maharashtra (Maharashtra State Innovation Society & Dept of Skills, Employment, Entrepreneurship and Innovation)

---

## 1. The Real-World Rural Challenge in Maharashtra

1. **Economic Vulnerability:** Livestock is the financial backbone of over 85 lakh farming families in Maharashtra. Diseases like Lumpy Skin Disease (LSD), Foot & Mouth Disease (FMD), and Hemorrhagic Septicemia cause catastrophic milk yield collapse, trade halts, and farmer distress.
2. **Late Detection:** Farmers often report diseases 3–7 days after infection onset due to rural distances, lack of awareness, and zero internet in remote grazing areas.
3. **Data Silos:** Field vets, diagnostic laboratories, and district administrations operate on manual registers and fragmented WhatsApp groups with zero centralized spatiotemporal intelligence.

---

## 2. The PashuRakshak Solution

PashuRakshak delivers a **GovTech / HealthTech intelligence platform** purpose-built for Maharashtra's rural reality:

| Problem in Field | PashuRakshak Innovation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **No Internet in Cattle Sheds** | **Offline-First PWA** using IndexedDB local queue with conflict resolution. |
| **Illiterate / Regional Farmers** | **Multi-lingual Voice Interface** (मराठी, हिन्दी) with speech recognition & Web Speech audio readout. |
| **Delayed Diagnosis** | **Explainable AI Risk Engine** providing instant 0–100 risk scoring & differential diagnoses. |
| **Unnoticed Disease Clustering** | **Spatiotemporal GIS Anomaly Engine** detecting $>3\times$ baseline deviations with Haversine buffer zones. |
| **Slow Lab Communication** | **Digital Diagnostic Chain of Custody** linking field specimens with automated case updates. |
| **Low Vaccination Penetration** | **Vaccination Tracking & Ring Campaign Manager** aligned with NADCP Phase IV. |
| **Basic Feature Phones** | **Simulated IVR 1800-180-1551 Phone Telephony** enabling voice hotline reporting. |

## 3. Measurable Impact Metrics

- **$70\%$ Reduction in Triage Time:** From report to vet dispatch within hours instead of days.
- **$100\%$ Offline Coverage:** Zero data loss during network blackouts.
- **Ring Containment:** Halts disease spread across district borders through instant radius advisories.
- **Govt Standard Design:** Built according to official Government of Maharashtra and MSInS healthtech design standards.
